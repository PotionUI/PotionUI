Make short videos with sound and multi-shot films with Google's Veo 3.1 models, without needing a graphics card.

Pick a model, describe the scene and generate. In image to video mode, drop the picture the video should start from into the Director's start well, and a last picture if you want the video to end on it. Switch the Director to several shots to make a film: each shot is one video, a shot can continue from the last frame of the shot before it or cut to something new, and the shots are joined into one video at the end. Veo makes 4, 6 or 8 second shots; 1080p and 4K shots are always 8 seconds long.

Every shot is made on Google's side and billed to the Google account of the administrator's API key, so a film of four shots is billed as four videos. Your prompts and any pictures you add leave this server to do so. A video that is stopped while it is being made may still finish there and still be billed; once you stop a film, no further shots are started.
