Make short videos and multi-shot films with hosted models from OpenRouter, without needing a graphics card.

Pick a model, describe the scene and generate. In image to video mode, drop the picture the video should start from into the Director's start well, and a last picture if the model supports one. Switch the Director to several shots to make a film: each shot is one video, a shot can continue from the last frame of the shot before it or cut to something new, and the shots are joined into one video at the end. The lengths, start and end pictures you can use follow what the chosen model can do.

Every shot is made on OpenRouter's side and billed to the administrator's OpenRouter account, so a film of four shots is billed as four videos. Your prompts and any pictures you add leave this server to do so. A video that is stopped while it is being made may still finish there and still be billed; once you stop a film, no further shots are started.
