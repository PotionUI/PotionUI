A toolbox for images you already have — cut the subject out, trim the framing to it, drop it on a canvas of your choosing, or key a flat colour back out. Nothing here runs a diffusion model: every stage is a deterministic transform, so there is no prompt, no seed, and no sampler. Feed it an upload or any previous generation from your history.

**Prepare for animation** is the mode the rest exist to support. Image-to-video models animate what is in the frame, and a character generated at 2048×2048 already fills that frame edge to edge — the moment it takes a step, swings an arm or turns, it walks straight out of shot. The fix is mechanical, and it has to happen in this order: matte the character *while it still fills the frame* (BiRefNet has the most pixels to work with, so the alpha edge is at its cleanest), trim to the subject's own bounding box so the framing no longer depends on where the generator happened to place it, then scale that subject down onto a canvas at your target video resolution with real padding around it. What the video model receives is a small, centred, correctly-framed character with headroom on every side.

The padding is filled with a flat colour rather than left transparent, on purpose: video models want opaque RGB, and an alpha channel handed to one is flattened to white (or black) by whatever touches it first, silently and without asking. Choosing the fill yourself means you know exactly what colour the background is — which is what makes it keyable again afterwards, with this preset's own **Colour Key** mode or in any compositor.

The other four modes are the individual stages, standalone: **Remove Background** (BiRefNet matte to an RGBA cutout), **Crop to Subject** (trim to the alpha bounding box), **Fit to Canvas** (scale and place onto a fixed canvas), and **Colour Key** (pull a flat background back out to alpha, with spill suppression). Matting is powered by BiRefNet (MIT licensed), vendored under `vendor/BiRefNet/`; the checkpoint itself is fetched like any other model through the admin downloader.
