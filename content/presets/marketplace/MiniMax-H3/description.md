Generate a short video and its soundtrack together from one prompt — the picture and the audio come out of the same pass, so the sound matches what is on screen instead of being added afterwards. Optionally anchor the opening and closing frames with your own images. Built for MiniMax-H3, which runs at a fixed 24 fps for 5 to 15 seconds and needs no guidance scale or negative prompt.

**Reference mode.** The second mode, *References*, generates a clip from up to nine reference images instead of from keyframes: the images say *who and what* is in the video (a person, a costume, a location, a style), not *where the video starts and ends*. Every reference reaches both the text encoder and the sampler, numbered by its position in the list — the first item is `<Picture 1>`, the second `<Picture 2>`, and so on — so the order you arrange them in is the order the prompt refers to them by, and reordering renumbers them. Give each item a label naming the subject it carries; the prompt is written against those labels.

Reference mode needs a **different DiT file** from the Video mode: the `ref2va` checkpoint partition, not the `fl2va` one. The two are identical in architecture and key set, so nothing in PotionUI can detect which one you selected — an `fl2va` checkpoint loads here without complaint and then produces a video that ignores your references entirely. Check the filename.
