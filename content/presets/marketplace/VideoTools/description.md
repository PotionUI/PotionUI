A toolbox for videos you already have — starting with frame interpolation. Drop in a clip (or a previous generation) and multiply its frame rate 2x or 4x, or bring it to a fixed rate (24, 25, 30, 48, 50 or 60 fps, from any source rate); the duration stays the same and the audio track is carried over. At a fixed rate, RIFE keeps the source frames that fall on the new rate, synthesizes each other frame at its exact instant, and shows the nearer frame across a cut instead of blending it. The RIFE checkpoint (RIFE 4.25, the recommended default, 4.26, or the older rife46–rife49 family) lives under `models/vfi/` and is fetched like any other model via the admin downloader. Interpolation is powered by RIFE (Practical-RIFE, hzwer — MIT licensed), vendored under `vendor/rife/`.
