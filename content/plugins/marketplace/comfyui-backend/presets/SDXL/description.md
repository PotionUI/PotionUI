Turn a text prompt into anime and illustration-style artwork. Built for Illustrious, a popular SDXL-based model specialised in anime characters, with a large library of community styles. Runs on your own ComfyUI server.

Illustrious checkpoints respond best to Danbooru-style comma-separated tags (e.g. `1girl, solo, ...`) rather than natural-language sentences — use the negative prompt to filter out common quality issues (`bad quality, worst quality, low quality, sketch, ...`).
