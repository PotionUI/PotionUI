Make short videos with hosted models from OpenRouter, without needing a graphics card.

Pick a model, describe the scene and generate. In image to video mode, add the picture the video should start from, and a last picture if the model supports one. Videos take a few minutes, and the controls you see follow what the chosen model can do.

Every video is made on OpenRouter's side and billed to the administrator's OpenRouter account. Your prompt and any pictures you add leave this server to do so. A video that is stopped while it is being made may still finish there and still be billed.
