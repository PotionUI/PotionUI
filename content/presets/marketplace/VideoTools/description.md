A toolbox for videos you already have — starting with frame interpolation. Drop in a clip (or a previous generation) and multiply its frame rate 2x or 4x: RIFE synthesizes the in-between frames, the duration stays the same, and the audio track is carried over. The RIFE checkpoint (rife46–rife49 family) lives under `models/vfi/` and is fetched like any other model via the admin downloader. Interpolation is powered by RIFE (Practical-RIFE, hzwer — MIT licensed), vendored under `vendor/rife/`.
