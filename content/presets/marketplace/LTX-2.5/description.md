Direct short video scenes — with optional synchronized sound — on a timeline where each part of the clip gets its own prompt, reference images or audio. A separate mode upscales finished clips to higher resolution. Built for LTX-2.5 from Lightricks, which ships its parts as separate files: a transformer-only DiT, a standalone video VAE, an optional audio VAE, and a Gemma4-12B text encoder that carries the text-embedding projection. For the earlier all-in-one LTX-2 and 2.3 checkpoints, use the `LTX-2 / 2.3` preset instead.
